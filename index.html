My Design Choices

I decided to make my portfolio about my soccer career at Binghamton University because soccer is a big part of my life. I chose green because it reminds me of a soccer field and my university. I used a light background with white sections to make the website easy to read. I included two photos of myself playing goalkeeper for Binghamton University to make my portfolio more personal. For my charts, I used a line graph to show my save percentage and a bar graph to show goals allowed in selected games. Overall, I wanted my website to look simple, clean, and organized.
