My Design Choices

For my portfolio, I decided to do it about my soccer career here at Binghamton. I did this because soccer is a big part of my life and would be the easiest to do work on. I chose green in my color choices because it reminds me of my jersey, and it's also the color of a traditional soccer field and the color for Binghamton. I chose to have a lighter background with some white sections so that the website would be easier to read without having such loud colors. I put two photos of myself from games this season because this data is about my current season, so it felt appropriate to have myself in the photos. I used a line graph so that I can show my save percentage and a bar graph to be able to show how many goals I've allowed in the selected games. The main goal was to make the website simple and clean while maintaining its organization.
